Common questions

Is a dirty condenser the same job as a compressor replacement?

No. A clogged condenser, a torn gasket, a bad controller, a leak, and a failed compressor are different lines. Ask which one they found before you authorize parts.

Why do walk-in quotes jump after the tech arrives?

A diagnostic visit is not a parts authorization. Ask for the measured box temperature and the named failure before a compressor line is on the ticket.

What certification applies if they open the system?

EPA Section 608 is the federal rule for handling refrigerant. Ask the shop whether the person opening the system holds it. This site is not the EPA.

Is a refrigerant top-off a leak repair?

Not by itself. Ask whether the quote is a leak search, a repair, or a recharge. This site does not set prices.

Who sets food holding temperatures in Philadelphia?

The Philadelphia Department of Public Health. Ask the shop what they measured, then confirm with the health department. This site is not the health department.
