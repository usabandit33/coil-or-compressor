A warm Philadelphia walk-in is not automatically a new compressor. Name a dirty coil, a door gasket, a leak search, or a compressor swap before you sign.

Restaurant owners authorize a compressor quote when the box is warm because of a clogged condenser, a torn gasket, or a leak that was never searched.

Ask whether the technician who would open the system holds EPA Section 608, and what box temperature they measured before food stays in the walk-in.

  • Ask whether this visit is a condenser coil clean or fan, a door gasket or closer, a refrigerant leak search, a thermostat or controller, or a compressor replacement.
  • Ask for the box temperature they measured and whether product should be moved. Confirm holding rules with the Philadelphia Department of Public Health.
  • Ask whether the person opening the system holds EPA Section 608. That is a federal refrigerant rule, not a city ranking.
  • Ask for the diagnosis in writing before a compressor line is authorized. A coil clean is not a compressor.
  • Ask whether a recharge is a leak search, a repair, or a top-off.
  • Call a shop that publishes a phone on its own website.
